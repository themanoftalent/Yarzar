## Overview

**Yarzar** (*Yarar & Zarar*) is a 100% independent, open-source health and ingredient scanner for macOS & iOS. Scan or look up food barcodes, cosmetic INCI formulas, or raw whole foods to get an instant **0–100 Health Score** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Mediocre`, `Poor`).

## How Yarzar Evaluates Products

1. **Food Products (0–100 Scale)**:
   - **Nutritional Quality (60%)**: Calories, sugar, saturated fat, sodium, fiber, protein, and fruit/vegetable/nut percentage.
   - **Nuanced Additive Safety (30%)**: Evaluates preservatives, colorings, emulsifiers, and sweeteners using **dose-aware concentration tiers** (`Trace <0.5%`, `Minor 0.5–2%`, `Significant >2%`) alongside EFSA, WHO/IARC, and FDA risk data.
   - **Organic Dimension (10%)**: Rewards officially certified organic products.
2. **Cosmetic & Beauty Products (0–100 Scale)**:
   - Analyzes every INCI ingredient for endocrine disruption, allergenicity, irritation, and regulatory caps.
3. **Whole Foods & Raw Produce Studio**:
   - Evaluates non-barcoded fresh fruits, vegetables, wild fish, nuts, and grains across **Glycemic Index**, **Clean Fifteen / Dirty Dozen pesticide cleanliness**, **key micronutrients**, and **optimal preparation methods**.

## Key Features

- **4 Scanning & Lookup Modes**: Live EAN-13/UPC barcode lookup (with OpenFoodFacts & OpenBeautyFacts integration + offline database), Live FaceTime Camera barcode scanner, Apple Vision photo/OCR ingredient scanner, and custom Ingredient List analyzer.
- **Smart Healthier Alternatives**: Automatically suggests higher-rated products in the same category with score delta comparisons.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix**: Compare any two products head-to-head.
- **Personal Dietary Guardian**: Custom alerts for Gluten-Free, Lactose-Free, Palm Oil-Free, Vegan, Low Sugar, Low Sodium, Endocrine Disruptors, and Fragrance Allergens.
- **Bilingual & Dual Theme**: Instant **English / Turkish (`EN / TR`)** switching and **Normal (Light) / Dark** themes.
